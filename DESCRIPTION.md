AhmedVPN Tether — the ultimate solution for iPhone users who need to route all hotspot traffic through an active VPN tunnel.

Unlike standard tethering where connected devices bypass the VPN and go directly through cellular data, VPNTether forces every packet from every connected client through your VPN tunnel. This means your laptop, tablet, or any WiFi device inherits the same IP, encryption, and geo-unblocking as your iPhone.

Perfect for:
- Privacy-focused users who need all devices behind the VPN
- Travelers using hotspot-connected devices in restricted regions
- Professionals requiring a single encrypted egress point
- Anyone whose VPN provider uses source-filtering (utun interfaces)

Features:
- Zero-configuration auto-detection of VPN and hotspot interfaces
- Kernel-level NAT engine with TCP/UDP/ICMP support
- TCP MSS clamping for optimal MTU across the tunnel
- No client-side setup required — works with any device that connects to your hotspot
- IPv4 NAT with full conntrack tracking
- Lightweight daemon with automatic restart
- Status display app with real-time monitoring (VPN, Hotspot, Relay, Connected Clients)
- Per-device license activation (SILEO edition) or unrestricted (HAVOC edition)

Requirements:
- Jailbroken iPhone on iOS 15+ (rootless, Dopamine)
- Active VPN connection (V2Box, NpvTunnel, or any utun-based VPN)
- Personal Hotspot enabled
